Niche font banks

Sprint 4, typography (section 2 of docs/sprint-4.md). Each bank pairs a display face (the wordmark, product names and page titles) with a UI face (everything else). All are free Google Fonts under the OFL. The colors are the app's current palette and don't change. Tap the chips to feel the selected state. The team picked Bank C (Bubbly). It's outlined below.
